Hospitalisations from Road Crashes
Trends Rates Data

Australia · 2011–2021

Road crash hospitalisations reveal a persistent and widening gap between First Nations and non-Indigenous Australians.

Using hospitalisation records from BITRE and population estimates from the ABS, this dashboard tracks rates of road-related hospital admissions across remoteness areas over a decade. The further from a major city, the starker the disparity.

Hospitalisation Trends Over Time

2011 – 2021

This chart tracks road traffic hospitalisation cases across remoteness areas in Australia from 2011 to 2021, comparing First Nations and Non-Indigenous populations over time. Use the filters to isolate specific groups or regions and observe how trends diverge — particularly in remote areas. Note that raw case counts are displayed, meaning population size differences between groups are not yet accounted for; refer to the chart below for a direct per-capita comparison.

Source: BITRE Hospitalised Injuries 2011–2021; ABS Estimated Resident Population.

Rate per 100,000 Population

By year & remoteness

This chart displays hospitalisation rates per 100,000 population, allowing for direct comparison between groups regardless of population size. Use the filters to explore how rates differ across remoteness areas and between First Nations and Non-Indigenous Australians for a given year. A significantly higher rate in remote areas reflects a range of contributing factors including distance from hospitals, road conditions, and vehicle types — and highlights where targeted policy action may be most needed.

Source: BITRE Hospitalised Injuries 2011–2021; ABS Estimated Resident Population.
