Population Data

Population figures are sourced from two ABS datasets. Projected Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander population figures provide annual estimates by Indigenous status, remoteness area, age and sex. Total population estimates by remoteness area cover LGA, Significant Urban Area, and Remoteness Area breakdowns. Non-Indigenous population is derived by subtracting the First Nations estimate from the total for each remoteness area and year.

Hospitalisation Data

Hospitalisation records come from the BITRE Hospitalised Injuries ongoing series. Three releases inform this dashboard, covering national figures, state and territory breakdowns, and a dedicated First Nations Peoples dataset — all extending to the year ending 2021. A data dictionary accompanying the series provides variable definitions and coding conventions not communicated directly within the data files.

Rates & Methodology

Hospitalisation rates are expressed as cases per 100,000 population. Crude rates are calculated by dividing the sum of hospitalisations for a given group, year, and remoteness area by the corresponding population estimate, multiplied by 100,000.

Rate = Hospitalisations Population × 100,000

These are crude rates and are not age-standardised. Comparisons across population groups should account for differences in age structure between First Nations and non-Indigenous populations.

Remoteness Areas

Remoteness classifications follow the ABS Australian Statistical Geography Standard (ASGS). Two adjustments have been made for this dashboard:

Dashboard label ASGS categories combined Reason
Regional Inner Regional + Outer Regional Aligns with First Nations population projection geography
Remote Remote Australia + Very Remote Australia Small population sizes; combined for statistical stability
